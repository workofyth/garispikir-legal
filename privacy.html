Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Versi Bahasa Indonesia di bawah

1. Overview

Garis Pikir Studio ("the App") is a private tool used only by the creator of the Garis Pikir channel (TikTok @garispikir5, YouTube @garisunderscorepikir) to publish the creator's own videos. It has no public users and does not collect data from the general public.

2. Data the App accesses

  • Authorization tokens issued by TikTok and Google when the operator connects their own accounts. They are stored only in a restricted file on the operator's own computer and are never sent anywhere except to the respective platform's API.
  • Basic profile information of the connected account (for TikTok: the open ID and display name returned by the user.info.basic scope), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Videos, captions and hashtags created by the operator, which are uploaded to the operator's own accounts.

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to upload the operator's videos and record a local log of what was uploaded. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or profiling, or used to train AI models.

4. Storage and retention

Everything is stored locally on the operator's computer. No server operated by the App collects or stores it. The operator can delete the stored tokens at any time.

5. Third-party services

Uploads go to TikTok and YouTube, whose own privacy policies apply: TikTok Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) and in the Google Account permissions page.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: GitHub Issues.

Kebijakan Privasi (Bahasa Indonesia)

Garis Pikir Studio adalah alat pribadi yang hanya dipakai pembuat kanal Garis Pikir untuk menerbitkan videonya sendiri. Alat ini tidak mengumpulkan data dari publik. Data yang diakses hanya token otorisasi TikTok dan Google (disimpan di komputer pembuat dalam berkas terbatas), informasi profil dasar akun yang terhubung, serta video, keterangan, dan tagar buatan pembuat. Data dipakai hanya untuk mengunggah video dan mencatat log lokal; tidak dijual, dibagikan, dipakai untuk iklan, atau untuk melatih model AI. Semua tersimpan secara lokal. Akses dapat dicabut kapan saja lewat pengaturan TikTok dan Google. Alat ini tidak ditujukan untuk anak-anak. Pertanyaan atau permintaan penghapusan: GitHub Issues.
